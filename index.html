FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

How do I get a Rudra Telecom connection?

Share your name, phone number, and location through our connection form. Our team will check availability and get in touch with you.

How do I know which plan is right for me?

Compare the speed, monthly data, price, and OTT benefits. If you're still unsure, contact our team and we'll help you choose based on your usage.

Do all plans include OTT benefits?

No. OTT benefits are available only with applicable OTT plans or eligible token allocations.

What are OTT tokens?

OTT tokens can be used to access participating entertainment platforms based on the token requirement for each service.

Can I buy additional OTT tokens?

Yes. Top-up token options are available on demand at the applicable rates.

What happens after I use my monthly data quota?

The connection continues at the applicable post-quota speed mentioned for your selected plan.

Is Rudra Telecom available in my area?

Enter your location or PIN code in the connection form. Our team can check availability for your area.

Can I change my plan later?

Contact our team to discuss available plan changes based on your location and requirements.
